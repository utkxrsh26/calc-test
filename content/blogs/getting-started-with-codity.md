## What is Codity?

Codity is an intelligent code review assistant that analyzes your pull requests with full codebase context. Unlike traditional code review tools, Codity understands your entire project structure, patterns, and history.

## Key Features

### 1. Full Codebase Awareness

Codity doesn't just look at your PR—it understands your whole project. This means:

- **Pattern Recognition**: Identifies when code doesn't follow your established patterns
- **Historical Context**: Understands past decisions and why they were made
- **Dependency Tracking**: Knows how changes affect other parts of your codebase

### 2. Intelligent Suggestions

Our AI provides actionable feedback that helps you:

- Fix bugs before they reach production
- Improve code quality and maintainability
- Learn best practices specific to your codebase
- Reduce review time significantly

### 3. Seamless Integration

Codity integrates with:

- GitHub
- GitLab
- Jira
- Slack
